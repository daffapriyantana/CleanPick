# Privacy Policy CleanPick

**Effective date:** 11 September 2026

CleanPick ("we", "our", or "the application") provides waste pickup
ordering services. This policy explains the data used to provide the service.

## 1. Data we collect

Depending on the features you use, CleanPick may process:

- Account data: name, email address, phone number, and account role.
- Pickup data: pickup address, pickup coordinates when provided, waste type,
  weight, vehicle type, pickup date, notes, and uploaded order photos.
- Order and payment data: order identifier, order status, payment status, and
  payment-provider transaction status. Payment card, bank, and wallet
  credentials are handled by Midtrans and are not stored in the application.
- Notification data: Firebase Cloud Messaging device token and notification
  preferences needed to deliver order updates.
- Technical data required by Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and
  messaging services.

## 2. How we use the data

We use this data to:

- authenticate users and maintain customer or officer accounts;
- create, manage, and assign pickup orders;
- show pickup locations and order details to authorized users;
- process and verify payments through Midtrans;
- send order, payment, and account notifications;
- protect the service against misuse and troubleshoot failures.

## 3. Services we use

CleanPick uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud
Messaging, and Midtrans. These providers process data only as needed for their
services and according to their own terms and privacy policies.

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with:

- authorized CleanPick officers when needed to fulfill a pickup order;
- Firebase infrastructure used for authentication, storage, and notifications;
- Midtrans for payment processing and payment-status verification;
- authorities or other parties when required by applicable law.

## 5. Security and retention

Data is transmitted using encrypted connections where supported by the
provider. We retain account and order data for as long as needed to provide
the service, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or maintain security.
Notification tokens may be removed when they are no longer valid.

## 6. Your choices and deletion requests

You may request correction or deletion of your account and personal data by
contacting the service operator. Some records may be retained where required
for legal, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

email before publishing this policy.

## 7. Children

CleanPick is not directed to children under the minimum age required by local
law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children for independent
use of the service.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy when the application or legal requirements change.
The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.
